Privacy Policy

PayMeBack for iOS and Android · Last updated 6 October 2026

Your name and details stay on your phone. Our servers only ever see the facts of your dispute.

This policy explains what the PayMeBack app ("PayMeBack", "the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. PayMeBack is published by Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited ("we", "us"), the party responsible for the data described under "What our servers receive". Questions: contact@cdandlc.com · +918214106158.

What stays on your device

These items are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded. Your identity details are kept in encrypted storage:

  • Your name, address, email address and phone number
  • The other party's name and address (landlord, employer, airline, merchant)
  • Booking, account, lease and employee reference numbers
  • Photos and documents you import as evidence (leases, tickets, emails). They are read on your device only.

Your case list (the de-identified facts you submitted and the resulting assessment) is also stored on your phone, in the app's private storage that other apps cannot read. Letters are generated on your device when you open them or export a PDF.

Your demand letter is assembled on your device by merging a template with these details. We never learn who you or the other party are.

What our servers receive

In the current version, nothing about your dispute is sent to us. Your claim assessment, statute references, demand letter and escalation checklist are produced on your device from a statute database that is bundled inside the app. We run no account system and keep no server-side copy of your case.

If a future version offers an online assessment service, it will send only de-identified facts, and this policy will be updated before that happens:

  • The dispute category (for example, security deposit) and your state or region
  • Amounts, dates and your answers to the intake questions
  • Your written description, after the app removes names, addresses, phone numbers, emails and reference numbers on your device
  • An anonymous session identifier, not linked to your name or account

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). They tell the app whether you own a purchase. We do not receive your name, card or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is genuine, versions of the app that have online purchase verification turned on send the signed purchase receipt from Apple or Google, and for a one-time letter a random case ID that contains no personal details, to our verification service. This contains no name, dispute information or device identifiers. We keep only a one-way hash of the receipt, so one letter purchase cannot be reused for many cases.

Check the preview. Before an assessment, the app shows you exactly what information is used on a preview screen. Automatic redaction is good but not perfect, and may miss some names or unusual identifiers. Review the preview and edit your description if anything identifying remains.

Retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device until you remove it. Delete everything at any time: open Settings → Delete my data. This erases your local profile, cases and reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes local data. Because we do not hold your name or account, we cannot look you up by identity. If an online assessment service is introduced, server-side case data will be deleted automatically after 30 days and "Delete my data" will also delete it immediately.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising ID
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • No sending of letters or contact with anyone on your behalf. You review and send every document yourself.

Security

Your identity details are stored in the app's private storage and protected by your device: iOS Data Protection (files are encrypted while the device is locked) and, on Android, encryption with a key held in the Android Keystore. Android cloud backup is disabled so your details are not copied elsewhere. No method is perfectly secure, so please keep your device locked and updated.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Because the app is designed so that we hold no direct identifiers, most requests are answered by the in-app "Delete my data" control. For anything else, email contact@cdandlc.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

PayMeBack is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
