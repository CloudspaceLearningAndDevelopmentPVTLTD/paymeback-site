Privacy Policy

PayMeBack for Android · Last updated 6 October 2026

Your name and details stay on your phone. Our servers only ever see the facts of your dispute.

This policy explains what the PayMeBack app ("PayMeBack", "the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. PayMeBack is published by Cloudspace Learning and Development Private Limited ("we", "us"), the party responsible for the data described under "What our servers receive". Questions: punith.nagaraju@cdandlc.com.

What stays on your device

These items are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded. Your identity details are kept in encrypted storage:

  • Your name, address, email address and phone number
  • The other party's name and address (landlord, employer, airline, merchant)
  • Booking, account, lease and employee reference numbers
  • Photos and documents you import as evidence (leases, tickets, emails). They are read on your device only.

Your case list (the de-identified facts you submitted and the resulting assessment) is also stored on your phone, in the app's private storage that other apps cannot read. Letters are generated on your device when you open them or export a PDF.

Your demand letter is assembled on your device by merging a template with these details. We never learn who you or the other party are.

What our servers receive

When you ask for a claim assessment, the app sends only de-identified facts:

  • The dispute category (for example, security deposit) and your state or region
  • Amounts, dates and your answers to the intake questions
  • Your written description, after the app removes names, addresses, phone numbers, emails and reference numbers on your device
  • An anonymous session identifier, not linked to your name or account
  • If you buy something, the purchase token from Google Play, used to confirm you own the purchase
Check the preview. Before anything is sent, the app shows you the exact request on a "What will be sent" screen. Automatic redaction is good but not perfect, and may miss some names or unusual identifiers. Review the preview and edit your description if anything identifying remains.

We use this data only to produce your assessment, statute references, letter template and escalation checklist, and to confirm purchases. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

Retention and deletion

Server-side case data is deleted automatically after 30 days. You can delete everything sooner at any time: open Settings → Delete my data. This erases your local profile and cases and deletes your server-side session immediately. Uninstalling the app also removes local data. Because we do not hold your name or account, we cannot look you up by identity. To request deletion by other means, email us and include the anonymous session identifier if you still have it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising ID
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • No sending of letters or contact with anyone on your behalf. You review and send every document yourself.

Security

Your identity details are encrypted on your device with a key held in the Android Keystore. Cases are kept in the app's private storage, and the app disables Android cloud backup so neither is copied elsewhere. Network traffic uses HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure, so please keep your device locked and updated.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Because the app is designed so that we hold no direct identifiers, most requests are answered by the in-app "Delete my data" control. For anything else, email punith.nagaraju@cdandlc.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

PayMeBack is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact
