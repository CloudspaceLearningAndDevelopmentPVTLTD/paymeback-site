Why does the app say it cannot assess my state or country?

We only show an assessment where we hold verified law. Other places return "more info needed" instead of a guess. Coverage is expanding.

Where are my letter and details stored?

On your phone only (identity details are encrypted). If you uninstall the app or choose Settings → Delete my data, they are gone and we cannot recover them.

I bought a letter but cannot see it.

Open the case from your cases list. If it still does not appear, try Restore purchases on the paywall screen, then email us with your App Store or Google Play order number.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete my data in the app. This erases your profile, cases and reminders from your device.

Report a problem with the law

Laws change. If you think a citation is out of date, tell us the statute and jurisdiction and we will review it.
